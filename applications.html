Research directions

What can you do with CheXthought?

CheXthought is a diverse resource. Below are some potential research directions you can explore (not an exhaustive list) for ways you can use our data!

If you end up citing CheXthought in your research, we want to know! and share your work so we can feature it on our website and build on it.

Vision & reasoning

Computer Vision & Reasoning Models

  • Fine-tune vision language models on expert chain-of-thought instead of final reports alone
  • Link each finding in the text to the image region it describes
  • Build visually grounded VQA from expert interpretations
  • Score intermediate reasoning steps, not only the final diagnosis
Report generation

Radiology Report Generation

  • Use expert reasoning as an intermediate step between the image and the report
  • Distill radiologist visual search into compact evidence for the report model
  • Control report sensitivity, such as thresholds for what gets reported
  • Train with preference or self-editing using expert reasoning as the reference
Agentic AI

Agentic AI

  • Build agents that zoom, crop and inspect regions before answering
  • Give agents tools such as retrieval, measurement and segmentation
  • Retrieval-grounded agents with auditable reasoning steps
  • Self-correction of unsupported findings
Clinical decisions

Sequential Clinical Decision-Making, Confidence & Calibration

  • Learn when to gather more evidence and when to commit to a finding
  • Study how the order of inspection affects the final diagnosis
  • Check whether model confidence tracks expert disagreement
  • Compare the confidence of model and expert in the same finding
Uncertainty

Uncertainty

  • Separate ambiguity in the image from uncertainty in the model
  • Identify images with more than one reasonable reading
  • Predict a distribution over findings instead of a single label
  • Conformal prediction sets for findings
Evaluation

Dataset & Benchmark Evaluation

  • Evaluate intermediate reasoning, not only final answers
  • Benchmark against multiple readers rather than a single label
  • Validate LLM-as-judge scores against expert ratings
  • Measure how much reasoning data a model needs
Failure modes

Model Failure Modes

  • Catalog misses, false positives and mislocalizations
  • Find cases where models are right for the wrong reasons
  • Compare model errors with the errors experts make
  • Test robustness across image quality and patient subgroups
Visual attention

Visual Attention: Human vs. Model

  • Compare model attention maps with expert cursor traces
  • Find regions experts check that models ignore
  • Compare the search order of models and experts
  • Test whether attention overlap predicts correct answers
Segmentation

Segmentation & Localization

  • Use cursor traces as weak supervision for lesion segmentation
  • Instruction-guided segmentation from expert finding statements
  • Localize findings without pixel-level masks
  • Compare predicted regions with the regions experts inspected
Reinforcement learning

Reinforcement Learning & Human Feedback

  • Design rewards for localization, accuracy and consistency
  • Learn a visual search policy from expert cursor trajectories
  • Train reward models on expert reasoning statements
  • Preference learning between competing interpretations
Interpretability

Explainable AI & Faithfulness

  • Test whether model explanations reflect the evidence in the image
  • Chain-of-thought faithfulness: does the stated reasoning reflect how the model reached its answer?
  • Counterfactual tests: does the prediction change when the evidence changes?
  • Attribute individual claims to specific image regions
AI safety

Hallucination, Safety & Chain-of-Thought Monitoring

  • Detect and reduce hallucinated findings in generated reports
  • Train monitors on expert reasoning to flag unsupported steps
  • Check each reasoning step against where experts looked
  • Detect shortcut learning from spurious image features
Human-AI

Human-AI Alignment & Trust

  • Map where model and expert interpretations differ
  • Teach models when to defer to a clinician
  • Study how explanations affect clinician trust and reliance
Longitudinal

Temporal Reasoning & Longitudinal Modeling

  • Detect clinically meaningful change from a prior study
  • Classify findings as improving, worsening or stable
  • Generate grounded descriptions of interval change
  • Measure consistency of interpretations across exams
Clinical NLP

Clinical NLP & Information Extraction

  • Study how radiologists express uncertainty in language
  • Extract findings, anatomy and their relations from free text
  • Classify statements as supported, contradicted or unresolved
  • Sentence-level image-text learning for zero-shot classification
